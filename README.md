# Research to Code

This repository is a collection of small, from-scratch implementations of AI research ideas, algorithms, and engineering concepts.

The goal is to turn concepts into code: one function, one test, and one clear explanation at a time.

This is not meant to be a full reproduction of every paper or a production-ready framework. Instead, it is a learning and engineering lab for understanding how important ideas work under the hood.

Each implementation is intentionally minimal, readable, and testable. Some entries may come directly from research papers, while others may come from common machine learning systems, library implementations, or practical engineering patterns.

The focus is on building intuition through implementation.

## Philosophy

A good implementation in this repo should answer:

1. What idea is being implemented?
2. What are the inputs and outputs?
3. What is the core math or algorithm?
4. What assumptions or limitations does it have?
5. How can we test that it works?

The long-term goal is to bridge the gap between reading research, understanding real codebases, and building reliable AI systems.
